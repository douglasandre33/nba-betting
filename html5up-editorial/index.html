How It Works

Player-Level Modeling

Minutes, usage, pace, opponent strength, rest, and injury context roll up to team projections and fair prices.

Line Shopping & EV

Edges are calculated versus multiple books; only +EV selections are posted.

Units, Not Dollars

Stake and P&L are tracked in units to keep results comparable for every bankroll size.

Full Transparency

Every pick is logged with odds, edge, stake and outcome. Review the current log or browse historical seasons.

FAQ

What’s a “unit”?

A fixed fraction of bankroll (e.g., 1 unit = 1% of bankroll). Tracking in units normalizes results across bettors.

How is ROI computed?

ROI = (Total Units Won) / (Total Units Staked). We show season ROI and recent windows (7/30 days).

When are picks posted?

Daily on slates with edges. If no edges today, we show the next scheduled date automatically.

Do we lose bets?

Of course we do. We will have losing days, losing weeks, and even losing months. At the end of the day, gambling with an edge is still gambling. Over the long run, we expect strong positive returns on our model.
